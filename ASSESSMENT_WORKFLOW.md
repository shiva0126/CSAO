# Assessment Workflow

## Analyst Console Flow

1. Open `Cloud Accounts`.
2. Add an AWS account and run `Test`.
3. Open `Settings` and confirm regions, collectors, logging, and report options.
4. Open `New Assessment`.
5. Complete the wizard sections for client context, scope, collectors, and output options.
6. Launch the assessment.
7. Monitor execution in `Assessment Progress`.
8. Review evidence in `Evidence Explorer`.
9. Validate findings in `Assessment Register`.
10. Review threat scenarios, correlations, attack paths, risk, and recommendations.
11. Preview, download, regenerate, or archive reports in `Reports`.
12. Reopen previous runs from `Assessment History`.

## Report Filtering

When analyst validation records exist, reports default to confirmed findings only.

## Cancellation

Assessment cancellation is available from the live progress page. Cancellation is cooperative and stops safely at stage checkpoints.
